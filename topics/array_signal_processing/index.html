Spacing Out: On the Reliability of Binaural Music Source Separation Metrics

Richa Namballa, Magdalena Fuentes

Despite the rising popularity of immersive audio, binaural music remains underexplored in music information retrieval (MIR), particularly regarding the task of music source separation (MSS).

查看文献提炼 基础摘要抽取

研究动机

Despite the rising popularity of immersive audio, binaural music remains underexplored in music information retrieval (MIR), particularly regarding the task of music source separation (MSS).

核心方法

  • While existing stereo MSS models can process binaural audio, they often degrade the spatial quality of the separated stems and undermine listener immersion.
  • In evaluating two alternative ITD estimation methods, we uncover a critical trade-off between robustness and accuracy, particularly for narrow-band instruments like bass.

主要结果

  • These results underscore the need for accurate, interpretable spatial metrics designed for binaural music to develop models that preserve source localization and listener immersion.

局限

  • Despite the rising popularity of immersive audio, binaural music remains underexplored in music information retrieval (MIR), particularly regarding the task of music source separation (MSS).
